# Swan Match — App Store Phase 1 Preparation

Swan Match is an **AI-Powered Marriage Platform**, not a dating app. This document supports Apple App Store review under Guideline 4.3(b) differentiation.

## Tagline

**Build a marriage. Not just a match.**

## Promotional Text (170 characters max)

Swan Match helps serious people prepare for marriage with AI compatibility, non-negotiable values matching, guided relationship milestones, and curated marriage recommendations.

## App Description (draft)

Swan Match is built for people who want a successful long-term marriage — not endless swiping.

**Marriage Readiness Assessment**  
Understand your strengths across family values, communication, lifestyle, finances, timeline, and long-term goals.

**My Non-Negotiables**  
Match on dealbreakers that matter for marriage — children, faith, lifestyle, relocation, and more — before you ever connect.

**AI Match Explanation**  
See why Swan recommends someone: shared values, marriage timeline, lifestyle fit, and long-term goals.

**Guided Relationship Journey**  
Move through clear milestones together: Introduction → Values Discussion → Compatibility Review → Ready to Meet → Relationship Ready.

**Today’s Marriage Recommendations**  
Receive a small set of highly compatible profiles each day — curated for marriage potential, not infinite browsing.

**Intent Verification**  
Periodically confirm whether you are seeking marriage, a long-term path, or need to pause matching.

Swan Match is for serious partnership. If you are looking for casual dating, this is not the right app.

## Screenshot Shot List (priority order)

1. **Marriage Readiness Report** — overall % + category stars  
2. **AI Match Explanation** — why this person is recommended  
3. **Guided Relationship Journey** — milestone progress (Introduction → Relationship Ready)  
4. **Today’s Marriage Recommendations** — 3–5 curated cards with “why recommended”  
5. **My Non-Negotiables** — dealbreaker selection during onboarding/profile  
6. **Intent Verification** — Marriage / Long-Term / Pause options  

Avoid screenshots that emphasize endless card swiping as the primary experience.

## Remaining low-risk renames (optional follow-up)

- Bottom nav label `Discover` → `Matches` or `Find`
- Interests tab copy that still says “match” in a casual sense → “marriage connection”
- Empty states that encourage “keep swiping” → “review today’s recommendations”

## Review notes for Apple

- Core product is marriage preparation + guided milestones, not casual dating.
- Matching uses hard dealbreakers and marriage readiness signals.
- Chat unlocks through a structured relationship journey after shared values work.
- Pausing intent removes users from recommendations.
