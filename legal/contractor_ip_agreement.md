# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS & WORK PRODUCT ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT
*This Agreement is entered into between Swan Match ("Company") and the undersigned independent contractor ("Contractor").*

---

## 1. Work Product Defined
"Work Product" means all software code, designs, algorithms, database schemas, documentation, graphic assets, mobile applications (iOS/Android), and other intellectual property conceived, developed, or created by the Contractor (either solely or jointly with others) in the course of providing development or consulting services to the Company.

## 2. Ownership & Intellectual Property Assignment
- **Work for Hire:** The Contractor agrees that all Work Product is considered "work made for hire" under United States copyright law to the maximum extent permitted by law, and all ownership rights vest immediately with the Company.
- **Absolute Assignment:** To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a "work made for hire," the Contractor hereby irrevocably, unconditionally, and permanently assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to such Work Product, including all copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, database rights, and moral rights therein.
- **Waiver of Moral Rights:** The Contractor irrevocably waives all moral rights, rights of integrity, or attribution under copyright law in connection with any Work Product created under this Agreement.

## 3. Further Assurances
The Contractor agrees to assist the Company (at the Company's expense) in securing, registering, and defending the intellectual property rights in the Work Product. This includes executing formal assignments, patent applications, or other legal documents necessary to record the transfer of ownership to the Company.

## 4. Pre-Existing IP
If the Contractor incorporates any pre-existing intellectual property (created prior to this engagement or owned by a third party) into the Work Product, the Contractor hereby grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, sublicensable, and transferable license to use, modify, distribute, and exploit such pre-existing IP as part of the Work Product.

## 5. Representations & Warranties
The Contractor represents and warrants that:
- The Work Product is their original creation and does not infringe upon the intellectual property rights, privacy, or proprietary rights of any third party.
- The Contractor has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and has not entered into any conflicting agreements that would restrict their assignment of intellectual property to the Company.
