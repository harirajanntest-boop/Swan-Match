# SWAN MATCH — STARTUP TRADEMARK FILING GUIDE
*A step-by-step roadmap to securing our brand equity, protecting the name "Swan Match", and owning the logo mark to prevent competitors or copycats from diluting our brand.*

---

## 1. What We Need to Protect
To secure full brand protection, we should register two distinct trademarks with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) or regional equivalents:
1. **Word Mark:** "SWAN MATCH" — Protects the actual letters and spelling of our name regardless of design, font, or color.
2. **Design Mark (Logo):** The specific visual logo mark (e.g. golden Swan match icon) used on our mobile app icon, splash screen, and website.

---

## 2. Choosing the Right International Classes
Trademarks are organized by "classes" of goods and services. For a mobile dating application and matchmaking platform, we must register under the following primary classes:

- **Class 9 (Computer Software):** Covers mobile application software (downloadable), dating app interfaces, and communications software.
- **Class 45 (Personal & Social Services):** Covers matchmaking services, online dating services, social networking services, and social introduction services.
- **Class 42 (Software as a Service - Optional but recommended):** Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) hosting, cloud database storage management, and platform-as-a-service.

---

## 3. Filing Status Options
When filing with the USPTO, we have two filing paths:
- **Section 1(a) - Use in Commerce:** Use this if the application is already live in the App Store/Google Play and we are actively facilitating matches and premium billing. We must provide a "specimen" (e.g., App Store listing screenshot and active checkout screens showing the "Swan Match" name).
- **Section 1(b) - Intent to Use:** Use this if the app is still in closed staging or beta and has not launched publicly. We secure the priority date today, and then submit a "Statement of Use" with specimens once we launch.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Filing Workflow

### Step 1: Perform a Comprehensive Trademark Clearance Search
Before filing, search the USPTO's Trademark Electronic Search System (TESS) to ensure there are no confusingly similar marks registered under Class 9 or 45:
- Go to [USPTO.gov](https://www.uspto.gov)
- Search for "Swan", "Swan Match", "Match Swan".
- Check for phonetic matches or similar logo graphics.

### Step 2: Prepare Filing Information
Collect the following specifications:
- Name of the owner (e.g., Swan Match LLC or Founder's legal name).
- Physical business address.
- Clear image of the design logo mark (in black and white or specific color constraints).
- Standard specimens (active App Store link, app icon screenshot, checkout page screenshot).

### Step 3: File the TEAS Standard Application
- Access the Trademark Electronic Application System (TEAS).
- Select **TEAS Plus** (Recommended: lower filing fees of **$250 per class**, requires choosing standard descriptions from the ID Manual) or **TEAS Standard** ($350 per class).
- Select Class 9 and Class 45.
- Enter the description: *"Downloadable mobile applications for online dating, social networking, and matchmaking"* (Class 9) and *"Online dating services; matchmaking services; providing a website and mobile application featuring technology that enables users to match and chat"* (Class 45).
- Pay the filing fees ($500 total for TEAS Plus across 2 classes).

### Step 4: Examination by USPTO
A USPTO Examining Attorney will review the application within 6 to 9 months.
- If there are no issues, they will publish the mark in the Official Gazette for a 30-day opposition period.
- If there are "Office Actions" (clarification requests or potential conflicts), we must reply within 3 months to prevent abandonment.

### Step 5: Registration
If no opposition is filed, the mark is officially registered! We can then legally replace the temporary `™` symbol with the registered trademark symbol `®` on our app store pages, website, and mobile interface.
