# DEVELOPER MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE & CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT
*This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Swan Match ("Company") and the undersigned independent software developer ("Developer").*

---

## 1. Confidential Information Defined
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information, source code files, database schemas, API keys, software architecture designs, product roadmaps, user growth metrics, ad-spend CAC ratios, business plans, financial projections, trade secrets, and user personal data shared by the Company (the "Disclosing Party") with the Developer (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the development and engineering services.

## 2. Obligations of Non-Disclosure
The Receiving Party agrees to:
- **Maintain Strict Secrecy:** Keep all Confidential Information in absolute confidence and prevent unauthorized disclosure to any third party.
- **Limit Use:** Use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing software development and engineering services for the Disclosing Party.
- **Restrict Access:** Restrict access to the Confidential Information to only those employees, sub-contractors, or agents who need to know the information to perform services and who are bound by confidentiality terms at least as restrictive as this Agreement.
- **Secure Data:** Implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to secure the Disclosing Party's source code, keys, and credentials.

## 3. Exclusions from Confidentiality
Confidential Information does not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known through no breach or fault of the Receiving Party.
- Was already lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance on the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
- Is lawfully disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party without any restriction on confidentiality.

## 4. Forced Legal Disclosure
If the Receiving Party is required by law, subpoena, or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, they must provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or waive compliance with this Agreement.

## 5. Return of Materials & Hard-Deletion
Upon termination of services or written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party must immediately return all physical documents and permanently delete all electronic copies of source code files, database snapshots, environment variables, credentials, and user data, certifying such deletion in writing within five (5) business days.

## 6. Remedies for Breach
The Receiving Party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure of source code, trade secrets, or proprietary algorithms will cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Therefore, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief to prevent or stop any breach of this Agreement, in addition to all other legal remedies.
