# SWAN MATCH — PRIVACY POLICY
*Last Updated: May 25, 2026*

Welcome to Swan Match. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and our platform services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection, transfer, manipulation, storage, disclosure, and other uses of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us Directly
- **Account Registration Data:** To create an account, you must provide your name, phone number, email address, gender, date of birth, sexual orientation, and matches preferences.
- **Profile Information:** You can build a rich profile by uploading photographs, entering your hometown, occupation, education, biography, and answering lifestyle or personality questions.
- **Identity & Age Verification Data:** To maintain safety and prevent underage usage, we collect facial photographs, ID documents (like driver's licenses or passports), and date of birth details during verification.
- **Messages & Chat Logs:** We process the content of chats and messages you exchange with other members of the platform to facilitate communication.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **IP Address & Device Identifiers:** We track your IP address, device brand, model, operating system version, and unique device IDs upon logging in to monitor security and throttle fraud.
- **Usage & Logging Information:** We log active session timers, click streams, screen views, and swiping activity.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We process your personal data for the following legitimate business purposes:
- **To Connect You with Matches:** Facilitate interest swaps, likes, matchmaking profiles, and active chatting.
- **To Secure Your Account:** Enforce brute-force login throttling, prevent spam bot generation, and protect logins with Firebase App Check.
- **To Detect and Mitigate Abuse:** Audit reports of harassment, auto-suspend accounts exceeding critical thresholds, and scan for duplicate photo uploads (catfishing).
- **To Facilitate Payments:** Process premium upgrades and manage subscription statuses via Stripe.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in the following limited cases:
- **With Other Users:** Your public match profile (photos, name, city, bio, and age) is visible to other users swiping on the platform.
- **With Service Providers:** We share necessary data with Firebase (for backend databases/notifications) and Stripe (for card processing).
- **For Legal Protection:** We will disclose data if required by subpoena, court order, or to protect user safety and defend legal liability.

---

## 4. Security & Data Retention
- **Security Protocols:** We store data securely in Google Cloud / Firebase. We manage production API keys and private keys in secure cloud Secret Managers.
- **Retention Period:** We retain your profile data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we perform a soft-delete instantly and permanently hard-delete all associated chats, files, and profile details after 30 days.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Choices
Under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:
- **Access & Portability:** Request a copy of all personal data we store about you.
- **Correction:** Rectify inaccurate profile or registration details.
- **Deletion:** Request that we erase your personal account data (easily triggered in-app via the Account Settings panel).
