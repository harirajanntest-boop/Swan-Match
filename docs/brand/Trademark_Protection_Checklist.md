# Trademark & Brand Protection Checklist

> **Step 22 — Brand / IP protection.** Practical steps to protect the "Swan Match" name and logo
> before you scale. Brand theft and copycat apps are common once a dating app gets traction; a
> registered trademark is what lets you enforce takedowns and shows investors you own your brand.

## 1. Trademark registration (USPTO — since the entity is Swan Match, Inc., California)
- [ ] Run a clearance search on the USPTO TESS database for "Swan Match" / "SwanMatch" to confirm availability.
- [ ] File a federal trademark application at uspto.gov for:
  - [ ] The **word mark** "Swan Match" (covers the name in any styling — most important).
  - [ ] The **design mark** (the gold swan logo / brand mark).
- [ ] File under the correct **Nice classes**:
  - **Class 45** — online social introduction and dating services (core).
  - **Class 9** — downloadable mobile application software.
  - **Class 42** — SaaS / hosting of an online platform (optional but recommended).
- [ ] Keep the specimen (screenshots of the app/site showing the mark in use) on file.
- [ ] Calendar the post-registration maintenance deadlines (Sections 8 & 15 declarations).
- [ ] Consider a trademark attorney or a service (e.g., LegalZoom/Trademark Engine) — DIY is possible but errors are costly.

## 2. International (when you expand)
- [ ] Since the app targets India (+91 / INR), evaluate filing in India (Controller General of Patents,
      Designs & Trade Marks) and via the **Madrid Protocol** for multi-country coverage.
- [ ] Register in any country where you actively market before competitors squat the name there.

## 3. Domain & handle protection
- [ ] Confirm ownership of `swan-match.com` (primary).
- [ ] Defensively register close variants/typos and key TLDs (`.app`, `.co`, `.in`, `swanmatch.com` if available).
- [ ] Lock down social handles (Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube) even if unused.
- [ ] Enable domain auto-renew and registrar lock to prevent expiry/hijack.

## 4. Logo & copyright
- [ ] Ensure the logo was created under the Contractor IP Assignment (see `docs/legal/Contractor_IP_Assignment_and_NDA.md`) so the company owns it.
- [ ] Keep master logo files (vector/SVG) and a brand style guide in the company drive.
- [ ] Copyright in original creative works is automatic, but registration strengthens enforcement — optional.

## 5. Enforcement readiness
- [ ] DMCA takedown process is published in the Terms of Service (DMCA agent: dmca@swan-match.com) ✓ — confirm that mailbox exists and is monitored.
- [ ] Designate one person to monitor the App Store / Play Store for copycat "Swan Match" apps and file takedowns.
- [ ] Keep a record of first-use dates and marketing materials (evidence of priority).
